## Recorded observation — RESULT-GENERATED / UNTESTED

- **Origin:** C1 development analysis (`ROUND1_REPORT.md`, 2023-01 → 2025-12 XAUUSDm M15).
- **Observation:** touches of $50 round levels **from above** showed fewer reversals than placebo levels,
  Δ ≈ **−9.9 pp** in the reversal framing (CI −17.4 … −2.9). That is the direction of a continuation or stop-cascade
  mechanism (Osler 2005).
- **Status:** **not evidence of an edge.** It was generated by looking at outcomes. It may become a hypothesis only
  if all of these hold:
  1. M1 or tick data materially resolves the M15 touch-ordering ambiguity (51% ambiguous on M15);
  2. the continuation mechanism is defined before testing;
  3. the first test uses data on which no continuation outcome has been computed. 2017–2021 qualifies: no C1
     outcome was ever computed there;
  4. the result-generated origin is disclosed;
  5. the preregistration is frozen before outcomes.

  The C1 development result is never confirmation, and the rule is not tuned on it.

## Why new data

- **Resolution.** M15 bars cannot order a level touch against the bar extreme.
- **Own-price only.** V1 and round 1 used only XAUUSDm's own price history.
- **Round 2 adds:** (1) XAUUSDm M1 and tick quality; (2) same-broker cross-market series.

## Local facts established (read-only inspection, 2026-09-29)

- **XAUUSDm M1 is cached.** The Exness-MT5Trial5 terminal holds XAUUSDm M1 base history (`.hcc`) for **2017–2026**,
  about 21 MB per full year. 2014–2016 files are about 90 KB each, the daily-only era.
- **XAUUSDm ticks are barely cached.** Only 2026-09 (one `.tkc` month). BTCUSDm has nine 2026 tick months cached, so

  EURUSDm or a USD index must be probed in the terminal.

## Candidate families (mechanism research; NOT approved hypotheses)

| family | mechanism / literature | data needed | prior for a gold-only algo |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Round-number continuation (stop cascade) | Osler 2005 (stop-loss orders just beyond round numbers cause cascades); the C1 observation is **result-generated** | XAUUSDm M1 (touch ordering) | moderate, *only if* M1 resolves the ambiguity; the first test must be on 2017–2021 |
| B. Gold–silver dislocation / lead-lag | 15-min gold–silver spread study: **silver bears most of the adjustment**, COMEX gold leads price discovery (*Applied Financial Economics* 2000) | XAGUSDm M1 | **low**: the literature says gold leads, so silver rarely predicts gold |
| C. Gold vs USD decomposition | gold co-moves with USD. Hypothesis form: the **USD-explained** part of a gold move persists while the **idiosyncratic** part (liquidity) reverts (common-factor vs idiosyncratic reversal) | EURUSDm (or a USD index) M1 | moderate: a genuinely external conditioning variable |
| D. Yield-conditional gold response | a strong but **regime-dependent** gold–real-yield link (Chicago Fed Letter 464, 2021; weaker post-2022) | intraday yields **not available** at the broker; external daily yields only | low (daily horizon, external feed, few trades) |
| E. Cross-market shock propagation | jumps and co-jumps in gold linked to USD, VIX and news attention (*IRFA* 2025) | EURUSDm / USD index M1 | moderate–low (probably simultaneous rather than lagged) |
| F. Quote / spread / liquidity state | spread widening and quote intensity as liquidity-state signals | real bid/ask **tick history** | unknown until the probe shows tick depth |
| G. Session transitions at M1 resolution | liquidity hand-offs (Asia → London → NY) | XAUUSDm M1 | low (close to rejected session-drift ideas) |

Power screening for each family runs after the audit passes, on counts only, as in round 1.
