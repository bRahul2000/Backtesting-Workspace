# A — Round-number continuation: preregistration (RESULT-GENERATED / CONFIRMATORY TEST)

## Origin — mandatory disclosure

The continuation idea was **generated by inspecting C1 development results on 2023–2025 XAUUSDm M15**
(`../ROUND1_REPORT.md`). There, touches of $50 levels **from above** showed Δ ≈ −9.9 pp in the reversal framing,
i.e. more continuation than placebo levels. **2023–2025 therefore cannot serve as confirmation.** This is the first
independent test, on **repaired XAUUSDm M1 for 2017-06-01 → 2021-08-31**, where no C1 or A outcome has ever been
computed. A pass is labelled **INDEPENDENT FIRST CONFIRMATION PASSED**, not "confirmed". A later final confirmation
would need a genuinely untouched sample, and any sealed sample requires Rahul's approval.

- **Mechanism.** Stop-loss orders cluster just beyond round prices. When price reaches a round level, triggered stops
  push it through the level: a price cascade (Osler 2005, *JIMF*; Osler 2003, *JF*). Approaching from above, sell
  stops below the level are triggered.
- **Before freezing.** The only execution was `a_study.py --dry` (event counts plus the cost scale): 377 round /
  814 placebo from-above events, and 376 / 798 from below.

## Exact specification (ONE; nothing else is decision-relevant)

| item | definition |
|---|---|
| round level | L = k × $50 |
| approach (primary) | **from above only**: M1 bar i with close[i−1] > L ≥ low[i] (the direction of the originating observation) |
| first touch / re-arm | the first such bar per (session, L); the session's first bar is excluded; no re-arm within a session |
| event timestamp | the M1 touch bar i |
| reference price | L |
| normalisation | d = Wilder ATR(14) of the **last completed M15 bar before** bar i's M15 bucket (M15 aggregated from the repaired M1). This is the C1 risk unit, known before the touch |
| continuation target | L − d (through the level); **reversal target** L + d |
| touch-bar rule | reaching the reversal target in bar i gives `ambiguous` (the extreme may precede the touch); reaching only the continuation target gives `continuation` (unambiguous: price must cross L) |
| horizon | bars i+1 … i+480 (8 h, the C1 horizon) in the same session. Both targets in one bar gives `ambiguous`; the first hit decides; otherwise `unresolved` |
| placebo (primary) | the C1 construction, unchanged: levels k × $50 ± 17.30 (+17.30 and +32.70 offsets), same rules, from above |
| duplicates | one event per (session, level, side); placebo and round levels never coincide |
| missing data | races stop at the session end; nothing is interpolated |

## Primary metric (one)

**Δ = P(continuation | round, from above) − P(continuation | placebo, from above)**, in pp. P = continuations /
(continuations + reversals). Ambiguous and unresolved events are excluded identically in both groups and counted.

- **CI.** A session-clustered bootstrap: 5,000 draws, seed 20261001, 95% percentile interval.
- **p-value.** One-sided (H1: Δ > 0), normal approximation Δ / SE_boot. It enters the Holm family.

## Non-decisional robustness (reported, never decisive)

- both approach sides pooled;
- from-below continuation;
- each placebo offset alone;
- $100 levels only;
- conservative (ambiguous counted as reversal, both groups);
- by year and leave-one-year-out (the latter is also a pass condition).

## Power, sample, practical threshold, cost

- **Expected sample.** About 377 round / 814 placebo from-above events, about 5% ambiguous.
- **Power.** Detectable Δ ≈ 2.8 × √(0.25 × (1/358 + 1/773)) ≈ **8.9 pp**. **Power at the 5 pp practical threshold is
  only about 30%, disclosed.** The originating observation was about 9.9 pp.
- **Minimum usable sample.** ≥ 300 resolved round from-above events, else the result is *underpowered — not passed*.
- **Practical threshold.** **Δ ≥ +5.0 pp**, the Gold V1/V2 bar.
- **Cost scenario, before outcomes.** There is no 2017–2021 spread data (the field is 0). The 2026 tick sample
  (median spread $0.264) is used **only as a scenario**. Against the 2017–2021 median d of $1.93, spread/d ≈ 13.7%, so
  a 1:1 continuation trade would break even at P(continuation) ≈ **56.8%**. Economic plausibility is judged after a
  structural pass. 2026 spreads are not claimed to be historical.

## Pass rule (first independent test)

**INDEPENDENT FIRST CONFIRMATION PASSED** only if **all** of these hold:
1. Holm rejection in the round-2 family (α = 0.05, one-sided);
2. Δ ≥ +5.0 pp;
3. ≥ 300 resolved round events;
4. every leave-one-year-out Δ > 0 (2017 … 2021).

Otherwise the result is **FAILED** (Δ ≤ 0) or **NOT PASSED**, and A is closed without rescue.

**If passed:** only non-sealed robustness and cost work follows, then **STOP** before any sealed sample.

**Forbidden:**
- $25 or $100 as primary, or other grids;
- the from-below or pooled direction as primary;
- other d values or horizons;
